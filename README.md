# 🛒 Retail Category Intelligence: Performance, Margin & Cross-Basket Analytics

An enterprise retail data analytics case study evaluating multi-category sales volume, profitability margins, customer purchase patterns, and cross-category affinities. Developed under **Calibo Major Use Case 01 (MUC01)** by **Team 13**.

---

## 🎯 Objective
The primary objective of this project is to analyze transactional retail records across multiple merchandise categories to identify profit drivers, eliminate margin leakage, and optimize product assortments. 

By integrating **individual granular category audits** with **unified group-level insights**, the project aims to:
1. **Identify High-Value vs. Low-Margin Categories:** Classify product categories into growth drivers, cash cows, and margin diluters.
2. **Uncover Cross-Category Purchasing Behavior:** Discover cross-selling and bundling opportunities to increase Average Order Value (AOV).
3. **Optimize Inventory & Pricing Strategies:** Formulate data-backed recommendations for pricing elasticity, promotional discounts, and supply chain allocation.
4. **Deliver a Dual-Tier Analytical Structure:** Provide both individual team-member analytical breakdowns and an executive group synthesis.
